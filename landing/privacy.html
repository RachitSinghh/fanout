Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Fanout Chrome extension and website (“Fanout”, “we”, “us”), operated by Rachit Singh. Questions: [support@your-domain].

The short version

Fanout sends each recipient their own individual email straight from your Gmail account. Your recipient lists and the content of your emails never leave your browser — they are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to us. If our database were breached tomorrow, it would contain no recipient addresses and no message content.

Google account access & Limited Use

Fanout requests only the minimum Google permissions needed to send on your behalf:

  • gmail.send — permission to send mail as you. This does not grant access to read, delete, or otherwise manage your inbox.
  • openid, email, profile — to identify your account (your own email address and name).

Fanout’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Specifically: we use Google user data only to provide the sending feature you asked for; we do not transfer or sell it; we do not use it for advertising; and no humans read it except where you explicitly ask for support, where required for security or law, or in aggregate/anonymized form that cannot identify a recipient.

Data stored on your device (never uploaded)

The following live only in your browser’s local storage (IndexedDB), scoped to your Chrome profile:

  • Imported recipient lists (email addresses, names, and any columns you add).
  • Your email subject and body, including personalization tokens.
  • Attachments you add, campaign settings, send progress, and per-recipient send logs.

This data is never transmitted to Fanout’s servers. You can remove it any time by clearing the campaign, or by uninstalling the extension.

Data we do collect

When you use the (optional, forthcoming) Fanout account and dashboard, our server stores a small, deliberately limited set of data — never recipient addresses, names, subject lines, or message bodies:

  • Identity: your own Google account email and identifier, to recognize you across devices.
  • Billing: subscription/plan status, handled by our payment processor (Stripe). Card details go directly to Stripe and never touch our servers.
  • Aggregate usage & diagnostics: counts only — e.g. “campaign X: 200 attempted, 197 sent, 3 failed” — plus error codes (never messages) for reliability. This is scrubbed of any recipient information in your browser before it is sent, and you can turn it off in settings.

Third parties

  • Google — authentication and email delivery (mail goes browser → Google directly; we are never in the mail path).
  • Stripe — subscription payments.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, and we do not use advertising or third-party trackers in the extension.

Data retention & your rights

On-device data is entirely under your control — clearing it or uninstalling removes it. Account and billing data is kept while your account is active; diagnostics are retention-capped. You may request access to or deletion of your account data at any time by emailing us. You can also revoke Fanout’s access from your Google Account permissions at any time.

Security

We keep our server surface small on purpose — the most sensitive data (your recipients and content) simply never reaches it. Payment data is handled by Stripe’s PCI-compliant infrastructure.

Children

Fanout is not directed to anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date. If we ever begin handling recipient activity (e.g. optional open/click tracking), this policy will be updated before that feature ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: [support@your-domain].
